Step 3: Managing Information (DML)

Once your tables are built, you need to manage the data inside them. DML (Data Manipulation Language) is what you will use 90% of the time in a real job to add, change, or view records.

Core DML Actions

  • INSERT: Adds a new row of data (like a new customer joining the bank).
  • SELECT: This is the "Search" tool. It allows you to find and view specific data.
  • UPDATE: Used when data changes, such as a customer updating their phone number.
  • DELETE: Removes specific rows from the table.
