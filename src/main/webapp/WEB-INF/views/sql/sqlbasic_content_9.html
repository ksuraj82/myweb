Step 10: Dividing Huge Tables

If a table becomes too giant, it becomes slow and hard to manage. Partitioning involves cutting one giant table into smaller, more organized pieces based on logic.

Common Methods

  • By Year: Keep 2023 data in one piece and 2024 in another.
  • By Region: Keep "India" data separate from "USA" data.
